# Power-board battery case comparison

Revision 2 of the October populated power-board study. Both supplied CAD formats are retained in `geometry/`. The STEP is the source for geometry registration. The case is 3D-printed PLA with air between the cells and the case. The two mounting scenarios use aluminium screws or a catalog nylon screw. The six existing battery-attachment screws retain their original specification.

The study covers original and refined default modal analyses, static bolt preload and locking, prestressed modes, X/Y/Z random vibration, seeded motion and stress histories, nonlinear edge-cell pull and the subsequent nickel-damage investigation. The [movie index](output/videos/README.md) links the reviewed deliverables. The paired delivery matrix in [video_pairs.csv](validation/video_pairs.csv) contains the 22 verified October movie references. Its planned deliverables comprise 22 bare-board movies and 22 matched cased movies showing both fastener variants. Nominal geometry and contact assumptions will be stated with the corresponding results.

The [October baseline notes](case-study-preparation.md) identify the full local Workbench archive. The isolated runtime under `.runtime/` holds generated decks, solver files and temporary fields. Native receipts are checked before accepting results for publication.

## Material references

The printed-PLA baseline uses the directional coupon properties and print conditions in the [PolyLite PLA technical data sheet](https://polymaker.com/wp-content/uploads/lana-downloads/PolyLite-PLA_TDS_EN_V5.4.pdf). The case bores are 5.000 and 5.090 mm, while the four PCB corner drills in the October ODB++ are 4.200 mm. The plastic scenario therefore uses an M4 × 0.7 × 12 mm nylon socket-head screw, McMaster-Carr 93640A145. The aluminium scenario uses the same nominal fastener geometry for comparison. Actual screw grade, fit, tightening, print orientation and infill are unmeasured. [McMaster-Carr metric screw table](https://www.mcmaster.com/products/nylon-allen-screws/)

The PCB hole centres span 62.865 × 114.427 mm, compared with 62.855 × 113.241 mm on the case. The centred nominal screw axes can be placed in the shared clearance of both bores, with little assembly tolerance along the long dimension. CAD intersections were calculated against all 35 mechanical solids in the archived battery assembly. With the tab faces flush to the PCB underside, each cell intersects about 1.70 cm³ of PLA. At 1.2715 mm stand-off the cells clear the case but four existing washers still intersect. Among the sampled offsets, 3 mm is the first to clear all 35 solids. It is an explored offset, not a measured spacer. The fit audit uses the midpoints of the hole patterns and the archived PCB underside at −0.55118 mm. [CAD intersection results](validation/case_fit.json)

## 8 November setup release

[Report preview](output/pdf/battery-case-report.pdf) and [Beamer preview](output/pdf/battery-case-slides.pdf) cover the two mounting positions, CAD interference and the fresh bare-board preload, modal and side-pull checks. Both default meshes reproduced all 20 October frequencies. The nominal edge cell reached 0.500 mm lateral travel under 8.542 N in the bare fixture. The global and local response campaign remains in progress. [Native preflight checks](validation/cased_preflight.json)

The first two bare-board modal movies were regenerated from the independently repeated refined-default RST. Both have 540 native-mode visualization frames at 3840 × 2160 and 60 fps. Their scalar field and visible deflection change with modal phase. The 6 mm peak motion is a display normalization. [Mode 1](output/videos/without_case/power_board_4k60/modal_fig01_default_mode01.mp4) · [Mode 20](output/videos/without_case/power_board_4k60/modal_fig02_default_mode20.mp4)

The initial contact audit found a false 7.685 mm penetration in the raised local fixture. Three bounded search radii all recovered a gap at both added contact pairs. The corrected raised local pull completed all 33 states. At 0.50 mm it required 138.53 N versus 8.54 N bare, with 112.15 N reaching the four nylon screw supports. At 8.54 N, the raised cell traveled about 0.21 mm. The fixture has fixed screw tops; vibration response is a separate comparison. The flush branch has genuine CAD interference and needs a separate installation check; a shorter search can hide part of the interference. [Quantitative checks](validation/contact_search.json) · [Report](output/pdf/battery-case-report.pdf).

The raised/nylon assembly now has a verified preload and prestressed modal comparison. Peak PCB motion during bolt tightening fell from 47.46 to 32.13 µm. Its three lowest modes are 253, 289 and 339 Hz; the original 781 Hz battery-motion shape still appears at 783 Hz. These are static and modal findings. Forced vibration response and flush installation are being tested separately. [Matched native shapes](validation/modal_pairs/stand3_nylon_basis.json) · [Updated report](output/pdf/battery-case-report.pdf).

For the 3 mm stand-off with nylon screws, the independent random-vibration calculation predicts larger out-of-plane PCB motion under each base direction. Under base X the PCB peak rises from 23.37 to 145.55 µm RMS; the peak cell displacement along X falls from 61.43 to 53.79 µm RMS. The method reproduced all 18 October bare-board displacement maxima within 0.013%. It uses fresh native mode shapes and independent frequency integration; the cased native spectrum solve and contact-under-vibration checks remain separate. [Bare check](validation/independent_psd/bare_basis.json) · [Raised results](validation/independent_psd/stand3_nylon_basis.json) · [Report](output/pdf/battery-case-report.pdf) · [Slides](output/pdf/battery-case-slides.pdf).

**Air gap under vibration:** the tightened raised/nylon assembly has only 0.143 mm of sampled clearance at the cells. During the October X-axis motion, the fixed-open linear model puts cell vertices as much as 0.214 mm inside the PLA at one sampled instant. The case therefore cannot be assumed to stay clear. The linear spectra compare assemblies; they do not give impact forces or the installed response after touching. [Geometry check](validation/clearance_rms/stand3_nylon_basis.json) · [Illustrated report, p. 10](output/pdf/battery-case-report.pdf#page=10).

The local flush/nylon installation stopped at 2.8685 mm of the commanded 3 mm. At that stage all four flexible tabs crossed the model's rigid PCB datum. The simplified fixture cannot establish the installation load or the subsequent side-pull response. The separate whole-board trial uses direct contact on the PCB and tab faces. [Partial native result](validation/partial_local/flush_nylon_lateral_friction_locked_approach1.json) · [Report, p. 11](output/pdf/battery-case-report.pdf#page=11).

Native contact submodels show how the raised nylon-screw case deflects beside the middle cell. At the same prescribed X-waveform state, activating contact reduced the largest sampled overlap from 0.2164 to 0.0445 mm in the wider domain. The required reaction changed by 16.2% between domain sizes. The model prescribes cell travel, so flight impact force remains open. [Report, p. 12](output/pdf/battery-case-report.pdf#page=12) · [Numerical comparison](validation/contact_patch_comparison.json).

The native bare X-axis random-vibration repeat matched all 17 October maxima, including the PCB equivalent stress of 90.325 MPa RMS. Four verified base-X camera orbits and three modal movies are available in the [movie catalogue](output/videos/README.md). The cased comparison panels are in progress.
