# Power-board battery case comparison

Revision 2 of the October populated power-board study. Both supplied CAD formats are retained in `geometry/`. The STEP is the source for geometry registration. The case is 3D-printed PLA with air between the cells and the case. The two mounting scenarios use aluminium screws or a catalog nylon screw. The six existing battery-attachment screws retain their original specification.

The study covers original and refined default modal analyses, static bolt preload and locking, prestressed modes, X/Y/Z random vibration, seeded motion and stress histories, nonlinear edge-cell pull and the subsequent nickel-damage investigation. The [movie index](output/videos/README.md) links the reviewed deliverables. The paired delivery matrix in [video_pairs.csv](validation/video_pairs.csv) contains the 22 verified October movie references. Its planned deliverables comprise 22 bare-board movies and 22 matched cased movies showing both fastener variants. Nominal geometry and contact assumptions will be stated with the corresponding results.

The [October baseline notes](case-study-preparation.md) identify the full local Workbench archive. The isolated runtime under `.runtime/` holds generated decks, solver files and temporary fields. Native receipts are checked before accepting results for publication.

## Material references

The printed-PLA baseline uses the directional coupon properties and print conditions in the [PolyLite PLA technical data sheet](https://wiki.polymaker.com/polymaker-products/more-about-our-products/documents/technical-data-sheets/pla/polylite-tm-pla). The case bores are 5.000 and 5.090 mm, while the four PCB corner drills in the October ODB++ are 4.200 mm. The plastic scenario therefore uses an M4 × 0.7 × 12 mm nylon socket-head screw, McMaster-Carr 93640A145. The aluminium scenario uses the same nominal fastener geometry for comparison. Actual screw grade, fit, tightening, print orientation and infill are unmeasured. [McMaster-Carr metric screw table](https://www.mcmaster.com/products/nylon-allen-screws/)

The PCB hole centres span 62.865 × 114.427 mm, compared with 62.855 × 113.241 mm on the case. The centred nominal screw axes can be placed in the shared clearance of both bores, with little assembly tolerance along the long dimension. CAD intersections were calculated against all 35 mechanical solids in the archived battery assembly. With the tab faces flush to the PCB underside, each cell intersects about 1.70 cm³ of PLA. At 1.2715 mm stand-off the cells clear the case but four existing washers still intersect. A continuous vertical sweep across all 35 solids locates first contact with the four outer washers at 2.397 mm of tab stand-off. The 3 mm exploratory mounting position leaves 0.603 mm of vertical travel before that contact. The physical spacer height remains unmeasured. The fit audit uses the midpoints of the hole patterns and the archived PCB underside at −0.55118 mm. ## 8 November setup release

[Report preview](output/pdf/battery-case-report.pdf) and [Beamer preview](output/pdf/battery-case-slides.pdf) cover the two mounting positions, CAD interference and the fresh bare-board preload, modal and side-pull checks. Both default meshes reproduced all 20 October frequencies. The nominal edge cell reached 0.500 mm lateral travel under 8.542 N in the bare fixture. The global and local response campaign remains in progress. [Native preflight checks](validation/cased_preflight.json)

The first two bare-board modal movies were regenerated from the independently repeated refined-default RST. Both have 540 native-mode visualization frames at 3840 × 2160 and 60 fps. Their scalar field and visible deflection change with modal phase. The 6 mm peak motion is a display normalization. [Mode 1](output/videos/without_case/power_board_4k60/modal_fig01_default_mode01.mp4) · [Mode 20](output/videos/without_case/power_board_4k60/modal_fig02_default_mode20.mp4)

The initial contact audit found a false 7.685 mm penetration in the raised local fixture. Three bounded search radii all recovered a gap at both added contact pairs. The corrected raised local pull completed all 33 states. At 0.50 mm it required 138.53 N versus 8.54 N bare, with 112.15 N reaching the four nylon screw supports. At 8.54 N, the raised cell traveled about 0.21 mm. The fixture has fixed screw tops; vibration response is a separate comparison. The flush branch has genuine CAD interference and needs a separate installation check; a shorter search can hide part of the interference. [Quantitative checks](validation/contact_search.json) · [Report](output/pdf/battery-case-report.pdf).

The raised/nylon assembly now has a verified preload and prestressed modal comparison. Peak PCB motion during bolt tightening fell from 47.46 to 32.13 µm. Its three lowest modes are 253, 289 and 339 Hz; the original 781 Hz battery-motion shape still appears at 783 Hz. These are static and modal findings. Forced vibration response and flush installation are being tested separately. [Matched native shapes](validation/modal_pairs/stand3_nylon_basis.json) · [Updated report](output/pdf/battery-case-report.pdf).

For the 3 mm stand-off with nylon screws, the independent random-vibration calculation predicts larger out-of-plane PCB motion under each base direction. Under base X the PCB peak rises from 23.37 to 145.55 µm RMS; the peak cell displacement along X falls from 61.43 to 53.79 µm RMS. The method reproduced all 18 October bare-board displacement maxima within 0.013%. It uses fresh native mode shapes and independent frequency integration; the cased native spectrum solve and contact-under-vibration checks remain separate. [Bare check](validation/independent_psd/bare_basis.json) · [Raised results](validation/independent_psd/stand3_nylon_basis.json) · [Report](output/pdf/battery-case-report.pdf) · [Slides](output/pdf/battery-case-slides.pdf).

**Air gap under vibration:** the tightened raised/nylon assembly has only 0.143 mm of sampled clearance at the cells. During the October X-axis motion, the fixed-open linear model puts cell vertices as much as 0.214 mm inside the PLA at one sampled instant. The case therefore cannot be assumed to stay clear. The linear spectra compare assemblies; they do not give impact forces or the installed response after touching. [Geometry check](validation/clearance_rms/stand3_nylon_basis.json) · [Illustrated report, p. 10](output/pdf/battery-case-report.pdf#page=10).

The local flush/nylon installation stopped at 2.8685 mm of the commanded 3 mm. At that stage all four flexible tabs crossed the model's rigid PCB datum. The simplified fixture cannot establish the installation load or the subsequent side-pull response. The separate whole-board trial uses direct contact on the PCB and tab faces. [Partial native result](validation/partial_local/flush_nylon_lateral_friction_locked_approach1.json) · [Report, p. 11](output/pdf/battery-case-report.pdf#page=11).

Native contact submodels show how the raised nylon-screw case deflects beside the middle cell. At the same prescribed X-waveform state, activating contact reduced the largest sampled overlap from 0.2164 to 0.0445 mm in the wider domain. The required reaction changed by 16.2% between domain sizes. The model prescribes cell travel, so flight impact force remains open. [Report, p. 12](output/pdf/battery-case-report.pdf#page=12) · [Numerical comparison](validation/contact_patch_comparison.json).

All three native bare-board random-vibration repeats matched their 51 October output maxima within 4.75 × 10⁻⁸ relative. All 12 bare RMS camera orbits and three modal movies have passed decoding and visual review. [Movie catalogue](output/videos/README.md).

Both 3 mm stand-off configurations completed the native bolt-tightening comparison. The peak PCB deflection was 47.46 µm bare, 32.13 µm with nylon case screws and 27.13 µm with aluminium case screws. The peak occurred at the same board node in all three assemblies. The case stayed clear of the cells, strips and washers throughout this load. [Report, p. 7](output/pdf/battery-case-report.pdf#page=7) · [Slide 5](output/pdf/battery-case-slides.pdf#page=5) · [Matched aluminium-case nodes](validation/static/stand3_aluminium_static.json).

Under the native X-axis random-vibration load, the raised nylon-screw case increased peak PCB out-of-plane displacement from 23.37 to 145.55 µm RMS and lowered peak cell-body X acceleration from 103.51 to 91.81 g RMS. Peak PCB equivalent stress fell 1.6% and moved about 54 mm across the board. Peak Y normal strain more than doubled. The native calculation holds the tightened contact state fixed. [Stress maps and comparison, p. 13](output/pdf/battery-case-report.pdf#page=13) · [All 17 native metrics](validation/psd_comparisons/stand3_nylon_X.json).

In the raised case under bolt tightening, aluminium screws lowered PCB deflection and raised the peak tensile stress across the PLA layers from 0.688 to 1.515 MPa. Under base-X vibration with nylon screws, layer-normal stress reached 17.16 MPa RMS within 0.53 mm of the modeled screw bearing. The local bearing and cell contact need resolution before predicting damage to a printed tab. [Printed-case results, p. 14](output/pdf/battery-case-report.pdf#page=14).

The raised aluminium-screw modal repeat recovered 48 verified modes. The first closely matched shape rises from 253.16 Hz with nylon to 320.17 Hz with aluminium. Under native X-axis base vibration, aluminium lowers peak PCB Z motion from 145.55 to 120.00 µm RMS relative to nylon, while the peak layer-normal stress near the case screw increases from 17.16 to 24.78 MPa RMS. The independent seeded response puts the cells in contact with the PLA at the instant of peak predicted tension at each variant's highest-RMS tab corner. [Screw comparison, p. 15](output/pdf/battery-case-report.pdf#page=15) · [Shape matches](validation/screw_modal_comparison/stand3.json) · [Peak-time geometry](validation/peak_contact/).

Four base-X subjects now have matched bare and cased RMS camera orbits. The cased movies show the 3 mm stand-off with nylon and aluminium screws on the same scale as the bare board. The highest nickel-strip stress fell from 291.9 MPa RMS bare to 256.2 MPa with nylon and 286.5 MPa with aluminium. Both raised assemblies still had cell clearance when the seeded elastic solution first reached the local study's assumed nickel yield stress. [Movie pairs](output/videos/README.md) · [Nickel comparison, p. 16](output/pdf/battery-case-report.pdf#page=16)

In the local flush nylon fixture, the first printed-PLA corner exceeded the reference in-plane coupon strength at 0.91–0.95 mm of screw-head travel. Through-layer tension crossed its reference at 0.99–1.03 mm. Both precede the later contact-penetration problem and the commanded 3 mm position. Actual failure depends on the print, bearing compliance and support geometry. Over the original 480-frame base-X movie interval, the independent signed-stress reconstruction gave peak dynamic PCB stresses of 154.41 MPa bare, 169.94 MPa with raised nylon screws and 151.03 MPa with raised aluminium screws. [Local fixture, p. 17](output/pdf/battery-case-report.pdf#page=17) · [Seeded stress comparison, p. 4](output/pdf/battery-case-report.pdf#page=4)

All seven seeded-motion subjects now have paired 4K60 movies of the bare board and both 3 mm screw variants. They cover three assembly views, three PCB displacement views and PCB stress. Each 8 s movie shows 29.24 ms of physical response. The time histories use independent integration on the verified native modes. All 22 bare subjects and 11 raised comparisons have been visually reviewed.
