# Power-board battery case comparison

[All 22 video pairs](output/videos/README.md) · [English report](output/pdf/battery-case-report.pdf) · [Seven-slide briefing](output/pdf/battery-case-slides.pdf)

The October power-board assembly carries three cells and six battery-attachment screws. Revision 2 adds the supplied 3D-printed PLA case, with air around the cells, and compares four additional case screws in aluminium or nylon. The model covers direct flush mounting and a 3 mm stand-off.

## Material references

The printed-PLA baseline uses the directional coupon properties and print conditions in the [PolyLite PLA technical data sheet](https://wiki.polymaker.com/polymaker-products/more-about-our-products/documents/technical-data-sheets/pla/polylite-tm-pla). The case bores are 5.000 and 5.090 mm, while the four PCB corner drills in the October ODB++ are 4.200 mm. The plastic scenario therefore uses an M4 × 0.7 × 12 mm nylon socket-head screw, McMaster-Carr 93640A145. The aluminium scenario uses the same nominal fastener geometry for comparison. Actual screw grade, fit, tightening, print orientation and infill are unmeasured. [McMaster-Carr metric screw table](https://www.mcmaster.com/products/nylon-allen-screws/)

The PCB hole centres span 62.865 × 114.427 mm, compared with 62.855 × 113.241 mm on the case. The centred nominal screw axes can be placed in the shared clearance of both bores, with little assembly tolerance along the long dimension. CAD intersections were calculated against all 35 mechanical solids in the archived battery assembly. With the tab faces flush to the PCB underside, each cell intersects about 1.70 cm³ of PLA. At 1.2715 mm stand-off the cells clear the case but four existing washers still intersect. A continuous vertical sweep across all 35 solids locates first contact with the four outer washers at 2.397 mm of tab stand-off. The 3 mm exploratory mounting position leaves 0.603 mm of vertical travel before that contact. The physical spacer height remains unmeasured. The fit audit uses the midpoints of the hole patterns and the archived PCB underside at −0.55118 mm. The initial contact audit found a false 7.685 mm penetration in the raised local fixture. Three bounded search radii all recovered a gap at both added contact pairs. The flush branch has genuine CAD interference and needs a separate installation check; a shorter search can hide part of the interference. The raised/nylon assembly now has a verified preload and prestressed modal comparison. Peak PCB motion during bolt tightening fell from 47.46 to 32.13 µm. Its three lowest modes are 253, 289 and 339 Hz; the original 781 Hz battery-motion shape still appears at 783 Hz. These are static and modal findings. Forced vibration response and flush installation are being tested separately. For the 3 mm stand-off with nylon screws, the independent random-vibration calculation predicts larger out-of-plane PCB motion under each base direction.
