MIT Rocket Team / simulation studies / 27 September 2026

OpenRocket & RASAero coefficient atlas

92 configurations, 82,800 common Mach/angle points, and 42 plot sheets. The installed MIT OpenRocket engine is unchanged. All RASAero sweeps were collected through its Windows GUI. Each comparison identifies the settings and any analytical reconstruction.
