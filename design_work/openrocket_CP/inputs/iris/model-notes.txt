The source simulation requests separation 5 s after booster burnout and sustainer ignition 10 s after booster burnout. In RASAero the sustainer delay is measured from separation, so both delay fields are set to 5 s.

OpenRocket loaded mass and CG from the nose: 73.2292 kg at 4.13688 m for the full stack, and 26.0760 kg at 2.41902 m for the sustainer. The mass calculation includes the recovery masses, camera shells and internal structure.

The native OpenRocket exporter omitted the booster when it encountered the stepped motor case. RASAero represents that stage with one tube. The model uses the 6.17-inch airframe/fin-can diameter across the booster cylinder, including the 41-inch region where the source motor case is 6.00 inches in diameter. It preserves the 5-inch interstage shoulder, 3.5-inch aft boattail ending at 5 inches diameter, 239-inch stack length, fin count, fin dimensions and axial placement. The 19-degree included leading-edge bevels use the RASAero Hexagonal Blunt Base profile.

The pairs of camera housings are represented by their combined exposed frontal areas as streamlined protuberances, 0.3694 in² on the sustainer and 0.4532 in² on the booster. RASAero calculates their drag from that approximation. The source pod drag overrides therefore do not transfer directly.

Launch settings follow the ORK: sea level, standard atmosphere, vertical 1-m guide, 2 m/s mean wind. RASAero uses a steady wind. Both nozzle exit diameters are set to zero because the supplied files contain no exit diameter. This omits the nozzle-area thrust correction with altitude. The source represents recovery equipment as mass components only, so the CDX1 has no deployed recovery device.

For the two short sustainer transitions, the as-run CDX1 retains the OpenRocket exporter’s diameter fields. The 0.25-inch record has Diameter and RearDiameter both 4.17 inches, and the 1-inch record has both 4.20 inches. Adjacent tube diameters remain 4.02, 4.17, and 4.20 inches. Explicitly entering the source forward diameters on those transition records produced a RASAero numeric overflow. Treat their local drag as an approximation.
