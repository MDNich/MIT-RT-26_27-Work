Source: [J. N. Nielsen, E. D. Katzen and K. K. Tang, NACA TN 3795, December 1956](https://ntrs.nasa.gov/citations/19930084523). The preserved PDF extract includes the tabulated results and geometry. [Full original scan](https://ntrs.nasa.gov/api/citations/19930084523/downloads/19930084523.pdf). The numeric transcription in `followup/tn3795.csv` uses the first (experimental) values in Table II and the areas, chords and semispans of Table I. Body-only measurements are +0.0340 and +0.0460 per degree, moment/lift slopes -0.20 and -0.20, in order of increasing Mach. Their reference area is the 1.50-in diameter body base, and their moment is about the nose, normalized by the 11-in body length. For wings and combinations use the full triangular area and the tabulated mean chord, with moments about the full wing centroid. Figure 1, printed page 29, establishes the aft tip station at 8 in (3 in ahead of base), the 3-in root chord at the body surface, 15-degree cone semi-angle and 11.02-in fairing radius. The wind tunnel Reynolds number was 5.5 million based on body length.
