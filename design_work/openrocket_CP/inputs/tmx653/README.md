Readings from the plotted experimental diamond (NB C Fl0) and triangle (NB C Fl10) markers; approximate rather than tabulated values. The three x positions are about Mach 1.97, 2.94 and 4.06. On the 1800-pixel-wide rotated scan of Fig. 4, the slope plot runs from approximately y=554 at zero to y=236 at 0.16/deg; the CP plot has zero at y≈698 and approximately 81 px per cylinder diameter increasing downward. The cylinder diameter is 1.25 in. The initial blunted cone has a 0.25d tip radius, 1.22d length and a 14°02′ cone angle. Both fixtures use a 2d center cylinder and 1.37d afterbody; the 10° flare ends at 1.48d. CP is measured aft from the nose-cylinder junction, coefficient reference area is the cylindrical midbody cross section. Scoring the difference at equal overall length isolates the flare increment. No fit uses these six readings.
