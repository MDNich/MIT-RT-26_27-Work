\req{SYS-01}{The application SHALL support all ten slots simultaneously. A
station number, role and vehicle mode SHALL come from the wizard or equivalent
CLI options. Discovery SHALL not silently change those choices.}
\req{SYS-02}{Each physical serial device or camera SHALL have one local owner.
Network clients subscribe to that owner's data. A second view SHALL reuse the
capture pipeline rather than open the same camera again. Duplicate camera
assignment and duplicate serial ownership SHALL remain prohibited.}
\req{SYS-03}{Launch Telemetry SHALL receive remote observations in addition to
its own board input. Remote data SHALL retain its source and SHALL not masquerade
as a local board connection. Away stations SHALL remain useful when either Launch
computer is absent. Launch Video SHALL operate independently of Launch Telemetry.}

The network consists logically of one LAN. The physical LTU-XR links, PoE hardware
and access points carry IP traffic; they are not telemetry sources by themselves.
An LTU-XR bridge and a Wi-Fi access point may be separate devices. The application
must not assume that an LTU radio itself implements a client Wi-Fi access point.

\section{Operator-facing requirements}
\req{UI-01}{Launch Telemetry SHALL show four station rows with distinct Telemetry
and Video computer cells, plus a separate Launch Video companion indicator.
Discovery, app heartbeat, hardware connection, RF reception and video freshness
SHALL be independently visible without changing tabs.}
\req{UI-02}{A cell SHALL expose station/role, IP endpoint, connection state,
last heartbeat age, protocol compatibility and mission identity. Details SHALL
include board state, packet age, observed loss/gaps, RSSI when provided, pointer
mode and commanded pose. Measured pose SHALL remain unknown when unavailable.}
\req{UI-03}{Network health SHALL use text plus color: online/green,
stale/amber, offline/red, undiscovered or inapplicable/gray. Conflict and
incompatible states SHALL be explicit. Rocket state colors remain the existing
independent convention: green GROUND\_TESTING, blue PREFLIGHT, red FLIGHT,
orange recovery/post-apogee, black other.}
\req{UI-04}{A connected pointer SHALL not imply a rocket RF link. USB frames
SHALL not imply receiver RF lock: a receiver may produce a no-signal screen.
Absent RF metrics SHALL display unknown rather than a fabricated quality score.}
\req{UI-05}{Launch Video SHALL display two large channels for Balius, three for
Iris, and all eight Away feeds as station-labeled thumbnails. Double-clicking a
thumbnail SHALL replace only its matching large logical channel. The operator
SHALL be able to restore local digital or choose another matching source.}
\req{UI-06}{Source selection SHALL remain visible on the large view. On loss,
the last image MAY remain with an unmistakable stale/offline overlay and age.
The UI SHALL not silently substitute another source. An unavailable main rendition
MAY show that same source's preview with a visible reduced-quality label.}
\req{UI-07}{Wi-Fi selection SHALL present the intended network and actual OS
connection separately. Permission denial, client isolation, blocked multicast
and inaccessible endpoints SHALL yield actionable status and manual-IP entry.
No public Internet connection SHALL be required for operation.}

\section{Data integrity, resource and packaging requirements}
\req{DATA-01}{Every accepted telemetry observation SHALL carry original bytes
when available, decoder identity, station/node identity, vehicle attribution
basis, mission, source mode, timing and reception metadata. Unknown values
SHALL remain unknown. Forwarding SHALL preserve provenance.}
\req{DATA-02}{The receive archive SHALL preserve observations independently of
UI refresh and network delivery. Multiple stations receiving one rocket packet
SHALL remain separate reception records but SHALL not count as independent
rocket measurements in a future estimator.}
\req{DATA-03}{LIVE, REPLAY and SIMULATION sources SHALL never silently mix.
Non-live network publication SHALL be explicitly enabled and conspicuously
labeled. The default subscription accepts LIVE only. This does not require a
new user-facing simulation mode in the application.}
\req{NET-01}{Telemetry/status and video SHALL use separate connections, worker
queues and resource budgets. A blocked media receiver SHALL not block serial
ingestion, event journaling, heartbeats or local controls.}
\req{NET-02}{All queues and messages SHALL have finite limits. Event loss SHALL
produce an explicit gap or disconnect, never silent success. Video SHALL favor
bounded live latency over displaying an ever-growing backlog.}
\req{PKG-01}{macOS and Windows packages SHALL contain the discovery, HTTP,
WebSocket, H.264 and SRT runtime dependencies. No separately installed media
server, Python, FFmpeg command, or Bonjour installer SHALL be required by the
operator. OS network/camera permissions remain normal platform requirements.}
\req{PKG-02}{Packaging SHALL verify actual codec, MPEG-TS and SRT availability
in the bundled binaries; finding an FFmpeg executable is insufficient. Software
encoding SHALL be available when qualified hardware encoding is unavailable.
Dependency licenses and notices SHALL accompany distributions.}
